Tracer markers

  1. Print this page at 100% / Actual size (not "Fit to page").
  2. Measure one black square. It should be 6 cm; if not, enter what you measured in Track › Marker size.
  3. Cut along the dashed lines and tape the markers near the corners of where you will draw.
  4. Keep at least two markers in the camera's view while you draw.
